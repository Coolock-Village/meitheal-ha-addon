# Code of conduct

Treat people with respect in Meitheal issues, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces. Give people room to ask basic questions. Describe the behavior you saw and the change that would help.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted contact, and disclosure of someone else's private information are unacceptable.

## Report a problem

Email [the maintainer](mailto:info@coolockvillage.ie). Include the link, what happened, and any steps that would help you feel safe. Share private details by email.

If your report concerns the maintainer, use the reporting controls on the service where it happened.

## What happens next

The maintainer reviews the report and limits who sees it. The maintainer can remove content, restrict participation, or ban a participant. The response depends on the conduct and its effect on others.
